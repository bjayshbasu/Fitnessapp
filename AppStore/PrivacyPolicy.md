# Workout Genie Privacy Policy

*Last updated: [DATE YOU PUBLISH THIS]*

Workout Genie ("the app") is made by [YOUR NAME]. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

## The short version

- Your workouts, foods and profile are used only to run the app for you.
- We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps and websites.
- You can delete your account and all your data from inside the app at any time.

## Data we collect

When you create an account and use the app, we store:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your name and email address | To create your account, log you in and reset your password |
| An account ID | To keep your data separate from other people's |
| Workouts, routines, exercises and personal records | To back up your training and sync it to your other devices |
| Food diary entries (food names, calories, protein, carbs, fat) | To back up your nutrition log |
| Optional profile details: photo, sex, birth year, height, body weight, goal and experience level | To show your profile and suggest calorie and macro goals |
| App settings (units, rest time, weekly goal, nutrition goals) | So your preferences follow you to a new device |

We do not collect your location, contacts, browsing history or payment information.

## Where your data is stored

Your account and backups are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which runs on Google Cloud. Firebase processes this data on our behalf to provide logins and storage. You can read Google's Firebase privacy information at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

A copy of your data is also kept on your iPhone so the app works offline.

## Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a food barcode or a nutrition label. Nutrition labels are read on your iPhone; the photo is not uploaded or saved.

## Barcode lookups

When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (https://world.openfoodfacts.org), a free public food database, to look up the product's nutrition. No personal information is sent with it.

## Apple Health

If you allow it, the app saves your finished workouts to Apple Health. The app does not read any data from Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising and is not shared with third parties.

## Sharing

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. Data is shared only with the services that run the app (Google Firebase, as described above), or if required by law.

## How long we keep data

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your login and all your cloud data are deleted immediately, and the copy on your iPhone is removed. Firebase may keep encrypted backups for a short period before they are permanently erased.

## Your choices

- **Edit or remove profile details**: Settings → your name.
- **Export your workouts**: Settings → Export history (CSV).
- **Log out**: Settings → your name → Log out.
- **Delete your account and all data**: Settings → your name → Delete account.
- **Turn off Apple Health saving**: Settings → Save workouts to Apple Health.

You can also email [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] to ask for a copy of your data or to have it deleted.

## Children

Workout Genie is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections, and each account can only read and write its own data.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
